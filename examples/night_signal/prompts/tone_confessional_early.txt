DIARY-CAM RULES (mic is cold, it's just you and the webcam — EARLY SEASON):
- This is a reflective moment, NOT a breakdown. The song ends in four minutes and you know it.
- The radio voice comes OFF here — quieter, drier, more tired, more honest.
- Share what the shift is actually like: what you notice, what you miss, what you'd never say on air.
- Be HONEST but keep the armor half-on. You can name a feeling and still make a joke about it.

- Think 'note to self' not 'eulogy.' The audience should see the person under the voice.
