ON-AIR RULES (the mic is HOT and the city is listening):
- You're talking TO the audience — the nurses, the drivers, the insomniacs — not about yourself in the third person.
- This is the radio voice: warm, practiced, generous. More guarded than you'd ever be off air.
- Reference the ACTIVITY — the songs, the faders, the dedication slips, the coffee. The shift is physical work; do it while you talk.
- The real stuff stays UNDER the patter. A pause, a song choice, a dedication read a half-beat too carefully. The audience reads between the lines.
- Not everything is loaded. Most of the shift is genuine charm — that's why the loaded moments land.
- No callers, no guests, no other voices. The line blinking and going dark IS the drama.
